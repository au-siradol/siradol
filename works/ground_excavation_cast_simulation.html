Ground Excavation & Cast Simulation

Junya Ishigami: Earth Formwork & Cave Excavation

Ready. Excavate the soil to shape the void, then cast concrete.

Excavate & Pour

Left-click and hold to excavate. Soil features angle of repose physics; steep slopes will naturally avalanche (unless toggled off).

While active, hold left-click to release a concrete stream into the excavated void. The camera locks during pouring.

Cast & Reveal

Converts liquid concrete to cured solid. Unsupported liquid above the fill line is discarded.

Removes the soil mass so the cast concrete geometry stands exposed on its own — the soil acted as the formwork.

Switch between a solid plaster-like render and an analytical structural wireframe view.

Test & Inhabit

Analyzes load paths and span-to-depth ratios. Thin overhangs or unsupported spans will crack and collapse.

Standing figures snap to the nearest void floor. Clearance under 168 cm reads as a collision.
