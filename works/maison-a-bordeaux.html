Model assumptionsAssumed elastic grillage · deformation relative to Normal day, display scale fitted automatically (real mm in the readout) · 80 kg/person · wine 1,000 kg/m³. Effective concrete E = 30 GPa, walls 0.33 m, hidden floor/roof diaphragms 0.20 m. Box torsion uses the cellular-deck constant (Hambly) for the walls + slabs as a closed tube. Relative wall stress is in-plane bending + beam shear only: it excludes torsional shear flow, bearing compression, local opening effects and cracking. Steel E = 200 GPa, effective I = 0.018 m⁴, tie A = 2,800 mm², pretensioned to 500 kN on a Normal day (lock-off ≈ 1,385 kN before the beam end and box deflect); bearing stiffness 1,000,000 kN/m. These are study assumptions, not surveyed properties. Stress colours: Normal-day peak = 40% of the scale. Lift motion is an architectural animation.
