Sources and limits

Classification and span ranges: Heino Engel, Structure Systems, pp. 62, 138, 176, 216 (read off his bar charts, ± one grid step). Depths, rises and sags are preliminary rules of thumb, cross-checked with Thomas Leslie's ARCH 445 lectures. Roof load is automatic: the system's own weight (a rough estimate from its type, material and span) + the roof covering + 0.10 kN/m² services + live load per the Thai Ministerial Regulation No. 6 (B.E. 2527) — 30 kg/m² (0.30 kN/m²) on roofs, 100 kg/m² (1.0 kN/m²) on concrete roofs. Wind, asymmetric load and buckling are not included; this is for choosing a scheme, not for design.
