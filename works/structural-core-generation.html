Safety Criteria

1. Structural Overlap (Punching Shear Risk):
Overlapping core areas (solid blue in 3D) are evaluated for load distribution. A minimum of 3 distinct support points or adequate footprint spread is required between floors.

2. Cantilever Span Limit:
The distance from the central structural wall to the furthest floor plate edge must not exceed the RC bending capacity calculated from the slab thickness.
