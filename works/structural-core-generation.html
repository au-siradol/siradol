The fold is the lateral system. One straight wall braces only along its own length; folding it gives wall area in both plan directions. Density is that area over the floor area it braces — a rule of thumb, not a code figure.

Order: Place → Auto-Fold → Align. Placing is axis-parallel, which is what pushes the wall aside; aligning first would remove the constraint that folds it. Aligning also brings each flight in to the wall, leaving only the slab band that carries the cantilever past the opening — running the stair hard against the wall face with no band is what would be structurally wrong.

Assumed: residential 2.0 kN/m², 1.05 m stair width (Swiss practice; no published drawing gives this building's figure), 2.10 m headroom, roof slab carries no opening.

The fold panel figure is a detailing guide, not a buckling limit — for a wall this stocky the critical panel length is tens of metres. Slenderness H/T_c is the real measure, and it is reported because it never governs at these thicknesses.
